# Fishus Maximus: the full game, implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The whole of Fishus Maximus as `Overview.md` and `Mechanics.md` describe it, with real PixelLab art throughout.

**Architecture:**
- **Rules** live in plain classes under `game/scripts/rules/`, with no nodes, so every rule is tested headless.
- **The world** is one coast scene, `game/scripts/coast/`, that builds any of the six places from its data file: parallax layers, a seabed depth profile, swimmers and the boat. The town, the shore (ponds and garum works) and the bay map are scenes of their own.
- **What carries over:** the two style tests already in `game/` are the starting point for the coast scene's drawing. Farmus Maximus supplies the patterns for saving, offline time, the desktop strip and settings, and Mosaicus Maximus the test runner, `Dev` flags, the fonts and `Nav`.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, 2D, GL Compatibility, 480 by 270 scaled by whole numbers. PixelLab through `tools/art/pixellab.py`. Python with Pillow and numpy for the art tools.

**Spec:** `Overview.md` and `Mechanics.md` in the repo root.

## Review Focus

- A hook cast past the seabed, or a boat rowed to the end of a place: the hook stops on the seabed and the boat at the place's end, never through rock or off the map (test in Task 3).
- The game closed in the middle of a fight or a trip: on loading, the day resumes from its last save with the hold, traps and ponds intact; a fish on the line is simply gone (test in Task 1).
- Time away of weeks, or a computer clock set backwards: at most 8 hours count, and negative time counts as none (test in Task 1).
- A fish landed with the hold or the live well full: the player chooses to release it or let another one go, and nothing is lost silently (test in Task 6).
- Switching to the desktop strip or resizing the window in the middle of a cast: the camera and layers fit the new view and the cast carries on (test in Task 10).

### Task 3: The coast scene

**Files:**
- `game/scripts/coast/`: `coast_screen.gd`, `parallax_layers.gd`, `seabed.gd`, `water_view.gd`, `boat_view.gd`, `coast_camera.gd`.
- `tools/art/check_edges.py`.
- `game/tests/test_coast.gd`.

**Interfaces:**
- **`class_name Seabed` (`RefCounted`):** built from a place's `seabed` points.
  - `depth_at(x: float) -> float`, interpolated between points.
  - `clamp_hook(at: Vector2) -> Vector2`, which keeps the hook in the water and above the seabed.
- **`CoastCamera` (`RefCounted`):**
  - `follow(boat_x: float, hook: Vector2, hooked: bool, delta: float) -> void`, `cam: Vector2`.
  - The camera leads the boat by 40 pixels in the direction of rowing. It follows the hook when the hook is more than 160 pixels below the waterline.
  - It stays within the place's width and depth.
- **`ParallaxLayers` (`Node2D`):**
  - `build(layers: Array) -> void`. Each layer is `{"file", "at": [x, y], "factor": [fx, fy], "z", "mirror", "band"}`; a band layer is the painted sea strip under a land layer.
  - `place(cam: Vector2)` positions every layer at `at - (cam - home) * factor`, rounded to whole pixels.
- **`check_edges.py`:**
  - Fails any image whose opaque pixels touch an edge across a run of more than 6 pixels, unless the image is listed in `tools/art/tiles.txt` (for things that tile, like sky) or the edge is the bottom of an above-water piece meant to sit on its sea strip.
  - Prints each failing file and edge.
- The coast screen uses the drawing from `coast_test.gd`: water by depth, rays that fade, glints, the waterline, the quay seen through the water, and the boat's hull tinted below the waterline.

### Task 5: Fish in the water and fishing with a line

**Files:**
- `game/scripts/rules/line_fight.gd`, `game/scripts/rules/bite.gd`.
- `game/scripts/coast/swimmers.gd`, `game/scripts/coast/line_view.gd`.
- `game/assets/art/fish/`: about sixty fish and twelve legends, painted without outlines.

- `game/tests/test_fishing.gd`.

**Interfaces:**
- **`class_name Bite`, static:** `chance(fish: String, bait: String, distance: float) -> float` (zero on a bait the fish doesn't like, falling with distance to 0 at 40 pixels), and `window() -> float` (`fishing.strike_window` = 1.0 second).
- **`class_name LineFight` (`RefCounted`):**
  - `setup(fish: String, pounds: float, gear: Dictionary, gentle: bool, rng)`, `step(reeling: bool, delta: float) -> void`.
  - Fields: `tension` (0 to 1), `distance` (pixels to the boat), `state` (`fighting`, `landed`, `snapped`, `slipped`).
  - **The fish:** it runs for 1 to 3 seconds and rests for 1 to 2, by its `strength` and `stamina` and the gear's `pull`.
  - **Reeling** raises the tension and brings the fish in; tension above the line's `strength` for 0.4 seconds snaps the line. Tension under 0.1 for 2.5 seconds lets the hook slip, sooner with a weak `hold`.
  - **Gentle fishing** never snaps and never slips.
- **`Swimmers` (`Node2D`):** spawns the place's fish from `Catalog.available` within the camera's reach.
  - **Movement:** fish wander inside their depth band, shoals keep together, and none crosses the seabed.
  - **Bait:** a fish within 40 pixels of a bait it likes turns to it and may bite.
- **The line in the game:**
  1. **Cast:** hold to fill a cast bar for up to 1.5 seconds, then release. The cast lands between 20 and 140 pixels from the boat.
  2. **Sinking:** the hook sinks at 30 pixels a second until clicked or stopped by the seabed.
  3. **The bite:** the float dips and the dog barks. Strike inside the window.
  4. **The fight:** a tension bar above the boat; hold the mouse to reel.
  5. **Landing:** on a catch, a card shows the fish, its weight and grade, and whether it is new to the logbook.

func test_gentle_fishing_never_loses_a_fish() -> void:

		while f.state == "fighting" and ticks < 9000:
			f.step(ticks % 2 == 0, 1.0 / 30.0)
			ticks += 1

### Task 6: Traps, nets, the trident, fishing on its own, and the hold

**Files:**
- `game/scripts/rules/traps.gd`, `nets.gd`, `idle.gd`, `hold.gd`.
- `game/scripts/coast/trap_view.gd`, `net_view.gd`, `trident_view.gd`.
- `game/tests/test_methods.gd`.

**Interfaces:**
- **`class_name Traps`, static:**
  - `fill(trap: Dictionary, hours: float, rng) -> void`: one catch per `traps.hours_per_catch` (2.0), up to the trap's `size`, drawn from the place's trap fish at the trap's depth.
  - `pull(trap) -> Array`: alive.
  - Each trap is `{"place", "x", "depth", "set_time", "catch": []}`.
- **`class_name Nets`, static:** `throw(shoal_size: int, net: Dictionary, aim_error: float, rng) -> int`, the number caught. That is between 1 and the net's size, and fewer the further the ring is off the shoal.
- **The trident:** night only. A throw hits within 6 pixels of a fish's centre. Speared fish are dead.
- **`class_name Idle`, static:** `catches(minutes: float, place: String, depth: float, bait: String, hold_room: int, rng) -> Array`.
  - It runs at a third of the active rate (`idle.rate` = 0.33 catches a minute times the active rate, 1.0 a minute).
  - It never returns a legend or a live fish, and stops at `hold_room`.
- **`class_name Hold`, static:**
  - `add(state, fish: Dictionary) -> String`: `"hold"`, `"well"` or `"full"`.
  - `freshen(state, minutes)`: fish lose `hold.fresh_loss_per_hour` = 0.08 freshness each hour of the day, and salted fish stay at 0.6.
  - `salt(state, index)`, `release(state, index)`.
- **A full hold:** a catch landed into a full hold or well opens a choice: release the new fish, or let one already caught go.
